# Experiences

Tell Claude about what you lived through, not only whom you met: a concert, a festival, a trip, a course. Claude keeps each as an **experience**: the day, what it was ("Konzert", "Kurs", "Reise"), a short title, a few lines, the place and who was there. Later you can ask "which concerts did I go to last year?" or "what did we do at the Olympiahalle?".

## Series

Something that recurs, like a dog-school course every Wednesday, is a **series**. Each lesson is an experience in it, on its own day. Claude starts a series only when you speak of something as recurring, and asks first. A series has a rhythm ("every 7 days"), a start, and an end once you say it's over.

When a session day passes and you haven't told Claude about it, freundebuch asks: in the [daily notification](notifications.md) the morning after ("🐾 Hundeschule am 04.11.: noch nicht erzählt"), and Claude brings it up when you ask what's due or open Erzählen. Everyone who was at a session of the series is asked, until one of you tells it. If you skipped a lesson, say so and that day isn't asked about again. When the series ends, the reminders stop.

Everything stays as a memory: an ended series keeps all its sessions, and nothing disappears on its own. You can delete a series only by asking for exactly that; its sessions then stay as single experiences.

## Places

A place has a name and other names, like a person ("Hundeschule Pfotenglück", "die Hundeschule"). Claude reuses a place you already have and asks when a new name is close to an existing one. A place's page shows everything that happened there; a series' page shows its sessions by date, with the missed and skipped days. Addresses and a map come later ([#78](https://github.com/Marceltov/freundebuch/issues/78)).

## Private and shared

Experiences and series are shared with your partner by default, like conversations; Claude asks before filing something sensitive as shared. A private ramble or a private series makes its experiences private. A place shows up for your partner only through something at it they may see.
