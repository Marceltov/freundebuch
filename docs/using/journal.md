# Journal

Tell Claude what is on your mind, not only whom you met: how the week went, what you're thinking about, what you plan. Claude keeps it in your **Tagebuch**, in your own words, and helps you see what runs through it.

## How it gets in

There is nothing to fill in. Ramble as you always do; Claude splits what you tell it into its parts:

- **journal entries**: what you think, feel or plan about your own life, dated, linked to the people you name;
- **conversations**: when you met, called or messaged someone;
- what you learned about people (facts, promises, dates, gifts, relationships).

All of them link to the same transcript, your words as you said them. On the web, a ramble's page (**Erzählung**) shows the transcript and every part filed from it; there you can also delete the ramble with everything that came from it.

## Private unless you share

A journal entry is **only for you**. Your partner doesn't see it, not in a count, a briefing, a reflection or the Verlauf, and a ramble with a private entry keeps its transcript private too. If you want your partner to read one, tell Claude to share it, or open the entry's menu on **Tagebuch** and share it there. See [Private and shared](privacy.md).

## Looking back

Ask Claude: "Wie ging es mir im Frühling?", "Was beschäftigt mich gerade?", "Was habe ich über Anna geschrieben?". Claude reads the entries of that time and tells you what it sees: themes, what changed, the people and conversations around them, quoting entries by their date. It reflects; it doesn't diagnose or give health advice.

In Erzählen, opened on no page, **Wie war meine Woche?** asks for it with one tap. A person's page shows your own last entries that name them (**Aus deinem Tagebuch**), and so does Claude's briefing on them.

## Weekly reflection

Under **Einstellungen → Wochenrückblick** choose a day and an hour. Once a week at that time, Claude reads the week's entries and your recent conversations and sends a few lines to your [notification address](notifications.md), linking to your Tagebuch. Nothing is filed. With no entries that week, nothing is sent. It is off until you turn it on.
