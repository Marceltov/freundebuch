# MCP tools

The MCP endpoint is `POST ${PUBLIC_URL}/mcp`: stateless, protocol version 2026-07-28. Every call carries the signed-in person's access token, and every tool acts as that person. Privacy is decided by the server, never by Claude.

Every call works in one book ([ADR 0026](../adr/0026-a-call-works-in-one-book.md)). `record` and `calendar_feed` take `book`, by name; it may be left out when you have one. A tool about a person or an item runs in that one's book. A tool that lists (`find_person`, `people`, `whats_due`, `search`, `journal`, `experiences`, `places`, `groups`, `traits`) answers once per book: `{ books: [{ book, … }] }`. `inbox`, `books` and `move` are about you across your books; every other result carries its `book`.

| Tool | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `record` | write | Files a ramble, its verbatim transcript, split into its parts: conversations (each with a summary, who was there, the place, and `at` the experience it happened at; a place may say `in` the place it sits inside, its `address` and its `owners`, whose home it is), experiences (a kind, a title, the place, who was there, and the series a session belongs to), [journal entries](../using/journal.md) (private unless `shared`), and the facts, promises, exchanges, dates, relationships and groups learned, each optionally naming the conversation it came from (`in`). A fact is `lasting`, a `situation` with a `checkAfter` day, or a trait (`need` or `personality`); a relationship says in words what each end is (`label`, `inverse`) and may go to `"me"` or `"us"` (a role: "our landlady"); a new person may get a gender. |
| `remember` | write | Files a fact, a date (a birthday), a promise, a gift idea, a person's gender, how they look (`looks`: the parts of their avatar) or where they live (`homes`: a place that is their home) about people already in the book, without a conversation. Facts take `kind` and `checkAfter` as in `record`. |
| `find_person` | read | Finds people by name or alias, tolerant of spelling, with context to tell namesakes apart. Claude calls it before referring to anyone. |
| `people` | read | Everyone in the book but you, by name, each with the groups you see them in and when you last saw them. With `withoutGroup`, only those in no group yet (never your partner), and `next` offers to sort them in. |
| `briefing` | read | Everything worth knowing about one person before you meet them: who they are to you (`roles`, read from your side: `us`, `me` or `partner`), what is going on (`situations`, with `nudge` once their `checkAfter` has come), what they need and how they are (`traits`), what else stays true (`known`), the one-hop `network`, and a suggested check-in interval. |
| `traits` | read | What several people need and how they are: any mix of people and groups, each person once, needs first. For planning something with several people. |
| `inbox` | destructive | Files you shared that wait under Prüfen: `list` (each with Claude's first read, its transcript and who is in it, and the files themselves as MCP content, five at a time, none over 1 MB) and `done` (close one with nothing to file; it is deleted). Filing with `fileIds` on `record` adds the files' transcripts to the ramble's and deletes the files: only text is kept. |
| `whats_due` | read | Upcoming dates, promises with a due date, overdue people and situations to ask about (`situation`); 14 days ahead by default. |
| `relate` | write | Records a relationship between two people, or between a person and `"me"` / `"us"` (a role: "our landlady", "my doctor"), or puts people in a group, outside of a conversation. The same relationship again, either way round or in another gender form, files nothing new. A group name finds an existing group by its name or another name (Kollegen → Arbeit). |
| `groups` | read | Every group you see, with its other names and how many people are in it. Claude calls it before putting someone in a group. `household` marks the household's own group (Haushalt to start with), which may be renamed but not deleted, merged away or emptied of whoever signs in. |
| `set_interval` | write | Saves how often you want to see a person, in days, or none. |
| `correct` | destructive | Marks done or outdated, edits (including a conversation's date and a relationship's words), deletes, changes visibility, renames, adds an alias, sets a person's gender (and returns their relationships to put in it), turns a fact into a relationship (`to_relationship`), merges duplicates, shares a ramble's transcript (by the ramble's or a conversation's id), deletes a ramble with everything told in it (`delete_ramble`, its recorder only); renames, adds other names to and merges places (`place_rename`, `place_alias`, `place_merge`), sets where a place sits and its address (`place_parent`, `place_address`) and whose home it is (`place_owner`, `place_unowner`); adds someone who was at a conversation too or takes out someone who wasn't (`participant_add`, `participant_remove`, its recorder only); marks a session of a series skipped (`series_skip`), and edits, ends (`edit` with `endsOn`) or deletes an experience or a series (its recorder only); `edit` with `{ kind, checkAfter }` moves a fact between lasting, situation, need and personality; takes someone out of a group, renames, merges or deletes a group and adds or drops its other names. |
| `experiences` | read | What you lived through (concerts, trips, courses and their sessions), newest first: in a period, of a kind, at a place, in a series or with a person; with the kinds and series in use. See [Experiences](../using/experiences.md). |
| `places` | read | Finds a place by its name or another name it goes by, tolerant of spelling; lists the places of a person ("where were we with Anna?", with how often and when last) or the places inside a place. Each comes with where it sits, its address and whose home it is. |
| `journal` | read | Your journal entries (and the ones your partner shared) in a period, the last 30 days by default, optionally only those naming a person; each with its day, text, people and ramble. |
| `search` | read | Finds what was told by a word you remember rather than by who or when ("Spiegel", "Hellengerst"): ramble transcripts, conversations, experiences, journal entries, facts, promises, exchanges, dates and places that hold every word of `query`, newest first, each with a snippet, the people, the day and a link. Plain word matching, case and umlauts folded, not meaning; only what you may see matches, never the other user's private items, and a transcript only when you may read it. |
| `get_transcript` | read | The verbatim ramble, by the ramble's id (`rambleId`, as `record` and `journal` return it) or one of its conversations' (`conversationId`). |
| `cards` | destructive | [Own cards](../using/cards.md): lists, writes and deletes your own cards (several, each with a name), makes or ends a card's link (a new link ends that card's old one), and subscribes a person to the card link they gave you, or drops it. Not in the in-app chat. |
| `birthday_message` | read | What birthday wishes to someone can be written from: the birthday, the age, who they are to you and what you know about them, never their health. With the final `text`, links that open WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS or email with it filled in; you send it yourself ([ADR 0023](../adr/0023-birthday-wishes-the-user-sends.md)). |
| `calendar_feed` | destructive | Gives you the secret URL of your [calendar feed](../using/calendar.md) for one book (`book`), making one when you have none; replaces or revokes it, which ends the old URL. Not in the in-app chat. |
| `books` | destructive | Your books, with your role and their members (`list`), a new book of your own (`create`), and, for one book named in `book`: `rename`, `invite` (a link that works once, for 7 days, as member or admin), `invitations` and `revoke`, `set_role`, `remove`, `leave` (what you marked private there is deleted), `hand_over` and `delete`. What each may do depends on your role ([ADR 0027](../adr/0027-invitations-and-roles.md)). |
| `move` | destructive | Moves people (by id, or a group's members) into another of your books, with what you may see about them; conversations, experiences and journal entries come along when everyone in them does. What another member keeps private stays behind with a remaining entry. Moved people get new links. |

## What a result tells Claude

Every tool result carries `next`: short instructions, built by the server from what this call did, that Claude follows before it answers you. It is empty when there is nothing to do. See [ADR 0010](../adr/0010-tool-results-tell-claude-what-to-do-next.md).

| Tool | What `next` says |
| --- | --- |
| `record` | Read back what was filed, part by part, with the day and the link, and say that a journal entry is private to you; who is new in the book; each situation's day, and that it is the six-week default when Claude gave none; a relationship that was already there and was kept; people who were in a group already. |
| `remember` | Each situation's day, and whether it is the default; for a gender it sets, which word of which relationship to put in it, as with `correct`; for a look, to say which parts were chosen and give the link to the person's page. |
| `relate` | A relationship that was already there (either way round, or in another gender form) and was kept; people who were in the group already. |
| `correct` | `outdated` on a situation: file how it ended as a lasting fact, if worth keeping. `edit` that makes a fact a situation or changes its day: the day. `merge`: what moved, and to look the person over for anything doubled. `gender`: which word of which relationship to put in the new gender. `to_relationship`: a relationship that was already there. |
| `find_person` | None found: ask whether this is someone new, then `record`. Several: ask which one, with the context that tells them apart. |
| `briefing` | Lead with the role ("Vermieterin of you both"); ask about each nudging situation first; say where a fact from a shared file comes from; offer the suggested interval. |
| `whats_due` | Suggest asking about each due situation; for a missed session of a series, ask how it went or whether it was skipped. |
| `experiences` | Answer from them with dates and links; for a series, how it went over its sessions. |
| `journal` | Interpret the entries: themes, changes, the people and conversations around them, quoting entries by date; reflect, never diagnose. None in the period: say so and offer another one. |
| `cards` | A new link only for you to pass on; a card that couldn't be fetched: tell you why; a fetched one: say what it says, and ask when its birthday differs from the one filed. |
| `birthday_message` | Draft a short message in your voice from one or two things they'd be glad you remember, show it, change it as you ask; with the final text, give you the links and offer to file it as a message once sent. No birthday filed: ask for it first. |
| `calendar_feed` | Give the URL only to you; an existing feed can't be shown again, so offer `replace`; a replaced or revoked URL has stopped working. |
| `search` | Answer from the hits with their days and links, reading the whole transcript when a snippet isn't enough; nothing found: try once with another or shorter word. |
| `get_transcript` | A transcript private to whoever recorded it: answer from the summary. |
| `move` | Read back who moved, with the new links; that what other members keep private stayed behind; what stayed because it is about people who stay. |
| `books` | `create`: the book is there; from now on name the book when filing. `invite`: give the link only to you, it works once for 7 days. Everything else: that it is done. |
| `set_interval`, `groups` | Nothing. |

The write tools also return what they filed (`filed` for `record` and `remember`, `relationships` for `relate` and `to_relationship`): each item with its id and link, each situation's `checkAfter` and `defaultCheckAfter`, and whether each relationship was new (`created`). `next` and these details are built only from what you may see: the other user's private items never show, not even in a count.

The server also sends Claude instructions with the connection, for what it needs to know before a call. In short:

- pass the ramble verbatim as the transcript,
- ask before filing anything sensitive as shared,
- resolve every person with `find_person` and ask when a name is ambiguous,
- follow the `next` of every result,
- take the day the user names ("vorgestern") as the conversation's date, and fix the date with `correct` when it was wrong,
- give you the link whenever you may want to look at something in the web app,
- prefer an existing group over a new one, and ask before creating one,
- file who someone is to you as a relationship to `"us"` or `"me"`, never as a fact, saying in words what each end is, in your language,
- file what is going on as a situation with an estimated `checkAfter`; agree a new day with you when there is news.

Every person, conversation and item in a tool result carries a `url` into the web app, built from `PUBLIC_URL` (or the request's host when it is unset). A person opens their page, a conversation its page. An item opens the page that shows it with its row highlighted: in a briefing or `whats_due` that is the person's page, in a conversation the conversation's page, which also lists done and outdated items. The REST API returns the same fields.

The exact input and output schemas are served by the endpoint itself (`tools/list`).

## Roles, situations, traits and gender

- **Relationships in words.** There is no list of types. Each relationship says what `from` is to `to` (`label`) and what `to` is to `from` (`inverse`), in your language and in each one's gender: Anna and her son Tom are `label` Mutter, `inverse` Sohn. The same words again, either way round or in another gender form ("Vermieter:in", "Vermieterin"), are the same relationship.
- **Roles.** "She's our landlady" is from her to `"us"`, `label` Vermieterin, `inverse` Mieter:in: one relationship per household member, each kept once. A briefing reads it from your side: "Unsere Vermieterin" only when you see it to both of you, else "von dir" or "von Sybille". See [ADR 0009](../adr/0009-household-members-in-the-relationship-graph.md).
- **Situations.** Moving, a new dog, looking for a car: a situation with a `checkAfter` day, estimated from how long it usually takes (six weeks when Claude gives none); the result's `next` has Claude tell you the day. News supersedes it and needs a new day, which Claude suggests and agrees with you; once it has passed, the situation nudges in the briefing, in `whats_due`, on Start and in the daily notification, until you tell Claude how it went. Then it is marked outdated, and `next` asks Claude to file the outcome as a lasting fact. Situations never disappear on their own.
- **Traits.** What someone needs (`need`: needs a wheelchair, vegetarian, doesn't drink) or how they are (`personality`: gets angry quickly, shy in big groups) is a fact of that kind, without a `checkAfter`. The briefing shows them as `traits` apart from `known`, and its `next` names the needs; `traits` gives them for several people and groups at once. Claude asks before filing a sensitive one as shared. See [ADR 0012](../adr/0012-traits-are-fact-kinds.md).
- **Gender.** `female`, `male`, `nonbinary`, or none: only when you say it or use a pronoun for the person, never guessed from the name. Claude writes the words in it ("Vermieterin", "Vermieter", "Vermieter:in" when unknown); setting it returns the relationships whose word for the person may need it (a first gender: only words still neutral, like "Vermieter:in" or "Kind"; a changed one: all of them), each once, and `next` tells Claude which word of each to put in the new gender.
- **Looks.** How someone looks, as the parts of a stylized avatar, not a portrait: `face` (oval, round, long), `skin` (light to dark), `hair` (none, buzz, short, side-part, curly, wavy, long, bun, ponytail, afro), `hairColor`, `glasses` (none, round, square) and `beard` (none, stubble, moustache, short, full), with your words as `description`. Shared like the name. Claude chooses the parts from what you describe with `remember` (`looks`), and changes one by sending the look from the briefing with that part changed. No picture is kept, only the choices; see [ADR 0020](../adr/0020-avatars-built-from-parts.md).
